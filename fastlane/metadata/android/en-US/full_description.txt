Mori is an offline comic reader for CBZ and CBR archives.

Your library links to the folders your files already live in — nothing is
ever copied or moved, and rescans pick up new files in place.

- Continue-reading shelf ordered by recency
- Favorites, in-progress / unread / finished filters
- Dual-page split for landscape spreads, with half-order invert
- Crop-margins reading mode and page-overview grid
- Fullscreen immersive reader with tap zones, pinch zoom, volume-key paging
- Material 3 Expressive interface with dynamic color and AMOLED black
- No accounts, no analytics, no network access: your files never leave the device
